MGT 409 — Final Project
joke of the day

I built five agents to run a shop.

The manager read the ticket, checked the stock,
checked the books, wrote the reply, and closed it out.

Then it filed a report saying it had consulted
Inventory, Accounting, and Customer Service.

It had consulted nobody. It did everything itself
and put four names on the memo.

Honestly? Most prepared for corporate life
of anything I've built all semester.
